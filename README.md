Juntos Website - Third Space Week 1 Project

## Purpose

Our project is a teacher review website for out highschool, inspired by Rate my Professors. We want to give students a place to share their experiences with teachers and classess so other students have a better idea to share their experiences with teachers and classes so other students have a better idea of what to expect. A course description can tell you what subjects you'll study, but it usually doesn't explain what a normal day in that class feels like, how much homework you might have, oor how the teacher approaches a difficult topic. Those are the kinds of details we want students to be able to share.

The idea comes from the questions students ask each other when choosing classes. How hard is the course? DOes the teacher explain things clearly? What should I know before signing up? Usually, the answers depend on who you happen to know. Someone with olderr friends might hear several different perspectives, while someone else might have very little information. Our goal is to bring those conversations into one place and make them easier for everyone to access.

We also want the feedback to be useful and fair. Different students cana have conpletely different experiences in the same calssroom, so one rating cannot tell the whole story. We hope students will explain what worked for them, what they struggled with, and what advice they would give someone taking the class next year. Specific comments about assignments, teaching styles, and expectations can help readers deccide what matters to them.

We're building this project for Third Space's first week, with the theme "harvest." Our interpretation is about gather what students have learned from their experiences and sharing it with the school community. We also brought the theme into design through autumn colors, original pixel art, and a walking turkey animation. We wanted the site to have some personality and feel like something made by students.

The project is still being developed, but the purpose is straightforward: help students learn from one another and feel more prepared for their classes. It is also a chance for our team to practice building a website together, combining programming, artwork, animation, and documentation into a project connected to our own school experience.
