About Harvest

A teacher review board, built by students.

This is a project for the 3rd Space program, and is meant to be a framework for a future school website. It is not meant to be a comprehensive tool, nor a draft for the future website. Instead, it serves as a way for us to look at what the vault has, and little other purpose.

The Idea

Real reviews, from people who actually took the class.

This will eventually be a website that will help serve as a place for students to write and post information about the school, helping all students, staff, faculty, and parents stay informed and ready for anything.

The end goal is to provide not only a unified place for high school students, but also to provide it without the need to accept predatory terms of service, subject yourself to addictive algorithms, or need to use artificial intelligence to get the information you need. We want to use our expertise to provide a place for students to be able to contribute and excel.

House rules

Be specific and fair.

Say which class you took, how the workload actually felt, and what you wish
you'd known on day one. Review the class, not the person.
