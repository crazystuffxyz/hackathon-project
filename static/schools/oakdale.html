About the School

Oakdale High School is a public high school located in Poppo County, USA. It's located in placeholder public county schools, and serves students from grades 9-12. It ranks as one of the top public schools in the state, and has great proximity to public transportation. Oakdale serves as a strong and diverse educational community, with 2.6k students, and a wide range of extracurricular activities. As a part of the CPS district, Oakdale has around 190 full time studendts, for around a 18 : 1 student teacher ratio.
